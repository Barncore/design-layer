# Critique rubric

Use only dimensions that matter to the artifact.

## Product and communication

- Is the artifact's single job apparent?
- Does hierarchy match user priority rather than implementation structure?
- Are labels, actions, states, and feedback specific and consistent?
- Does the design handle absence, failure, waiting, success, and risk honestly?

## Visual language

- Does the form grow from the subject, product, and audience?
- Is there a coherent system of type, color, spacing, surfaces, imagery, and motion?
- Is the signature idea memorable and earned, or merely decorative?
- Is expressive energy concentrated deliberately or sprayed everywhere?

## Interaction and spatial logic

- Are controls legible as controls and states legible as states?
- Does motion explain, confirm, or preserve continuity?
- Does spatial organization remain coherent across viewports and states?

## Taste fit

Label the evidence source:

- project: approved `DESIGN.md` or project overlay
- contextual: preference demonstrated in this domain or task
- global: broader taste profile

State confidence and contradictory evidence. Aesthetic recipes inferred from prose remain hypotheses until visual examples confirm them.

## Prioritization

Prioritize by leverage, not annoyance. One hierarchy correction may matter more than ten microscopic polish notes. Preserve strengths that carry identity or comprehension.
