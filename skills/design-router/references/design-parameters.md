# Design parameters: a working field guide

Use this repertoire when choosing or refining a visual or interaction direction, including when a brief implies qualities without naming a technique. It contains all 86 dimensions from the original field guide, organised into 12 domains. Read the domains relevant to the actual design decision; the entire catalogue need not be loaded for every task.

## Turn the brief into choices

Choose the dimensions that could materially express the subject, audience, intended experience and use. Combine their settings into a coherent direction and recommend it. For example, an intimate image sequence might connect subject distance, focal crop, reading measure and narrative pacing; a comparison workspace might connect density distribution, view switching and recovery. The brief can justify very different treatments within either kind of project.

The ranges are starting points, not binary choices, universal defaults or a checklist to maximise. Explore combinations beyond the examples. Keep the accepted creative ambition intact: delight, atmosphere, tension and play can be reasons for a choice. Existing project decisions and explicit instructions remain authoritative.

Make these judgments yourself from project context. Ask the user about a consequential gap in intent, or show a focused comparison when it helps them react; do not turn the catalogue into a parameter survey. In the existing brief or rationale, carry only the consequential choices, their project-specific reasons and unresolved uncertainty. No separate 86-row decision ledger is required.

During implementation or critique, revisit the selected relationships in real content, states and viewports. If the result does not serve the intended experience, adjust the relevant dimensions together rather than adding unrelated effects. Requirements such as readable contrast and usable input remain separate from subjective preferences.

## Evidence and deeper mechanics

The dimensions are design vocabulary and original synthesis, not empirical laws or stored personal preferences. The [source guide](design-parameters/sources.md) preserves all 44 public references with their roles and observation prompts. Original source review dates do not establish current browser support or present-day site behaviour. No personal ratings, reaction notes or media collection is included.

Each domain links to more detailed implementation references where available. Use those for mechanics such as gamut, direct manipulation and rendering; use this field guide to decide which relationships the project needs. [Coverage metadata](design-parameters/manifest.json) preserves stable IDs so the package validator can detect missing dimensions or broken routes.
