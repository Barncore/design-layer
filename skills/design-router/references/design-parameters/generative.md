# Rules & controlled surprise

A coherent family of outcomes rather than one frozen composition.

Use these dimensions to make project-specific choices. The alternatives are examples to combine or extend, not scores or required opposites. Choose from the brief and inspect the consequences; the user does not need to answer each dimension.

## generative-01: Rule visibility

**Alternatives:** Hidden algorithm / visible construction

**What changes:** A repeated relation can make many outputs feel like one identity.

**Apply to the brief:** Define the relation that unifies outputs and choose whether exposing its construction helps the audience understand or enjoy the work.

## generative-02: Order and entropy

**Alternatives:** Regular / perturbed / turbulent

**What changes:** Randomness has amount, scale and distribution, not just an on switch.

**Apply to the brief:** Control the amount, spatial scale and distribution of variation; compare recognisability and hierarchy as entropy increases.

## generative-03: Geometry family

**Alternatives:** Cells / fields / particles / lines / volumes

**What changes:** Each family makes different relationships easy to express.

**Apply to the brief:** Choose a form family whose relationships express the subject, then explore its parameters instead of collecting unrelated effects.

## generative-04: Input relationship

**Alternatives:** Time / pointer / sound / content / data

**What changes:** The input can explain why the output changes.

**Apply to the brief:** Connect the changing output to a meaningful input where causality is part of the experience; independent ambient behaviour can also be intentional.

## generative-05: Reproducibility

**Alternatives:** Fixed seed / session variation / continually changing

**What changes:** A system can be varied while retaining a stable identity.

**Apply to the brief:** Choose stable seeds, session variation or continuous change according to what must remain recognisable, reproducible or comparable.

## generative-06: Semantic connection

**Alternatives:** Decorative system / subject-derived rule

**What changes:** A visual process can embody something true about the content.

**Apply to the brief:** Derive the generation rule from the subject when it strengthens identity; atmosphere and play can also justify a system when their purpose is clear.
