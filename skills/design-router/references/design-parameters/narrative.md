# Sequence & revelation

How an experience moves between curiosity, understanding and action.

Use these dimensions to make project-specific choices. The alternatives are examples to combine or extend, not scores or required opposites. Choose from the brief and inspect the consequences; the user does not need to answer each dimension.

## narrative-01: Pacing

**Alternatives:** Continuous flow / chapters / deliberate pauses

**What changes:** The distance and action between ideas controls their perceived relationship.

**Apply to the brief:** Use chapters, continuity or pauses to express the relationship between ideas; allow a slower encounter without unnecessarily forcing waiting.

## narrative-02: Reveal logic

**Alternatives:** Everything upfront / progressive evidence / surprise

**What changes:** A reveal should change understanding, not merely delay content.

**Apply to the brief:** Reveal information when its order changes understanding or rewards discovery; keep essential task information available when delay adds no value.

## narrative-03: Control of time

**Alternatives:** Native scroll / scroll-linked scene / user scrub

**What changes:** A timeline can be read, played or manipulated.

**Apply to the brief:** Choose scrolling, playback or scrubbing according to how the audience should control the sequence; preserve orientation on fast movement, reversal and alternate input.

## narrative-04: Overview and detail

**Alternatives:** Separate pages / linked scales / simultaneous view

**What changes:** A detail can stay connected to the whole instead of replacing it.

**Apply to the brief:** Keep detail linked to overview when comparison or causal understanding benefits; choose simultaneous or separate views according to available space and attention.

## narrative-05: Tone shifts

**Alternatives:** Consistent register / contrast and release

**What changes:** A serious explanation can include a small playful turn.

**Apply to the brief:** Let a shift in register express a meaningful turn in the content; assess whether play, gravity or release strengthens the whole experience.

## narrative-06: Ending

**Alternatives:** Abrupt finish / useful action / open exploration

**What changes:** The last state decides what the visitor can do with the experience.

**Apply to the brief:** Design the final state around what the visitor can now do or understand, whether that means a useful action, closure or continuing exploration.
