# Interaction & agency

What people can manipulate, discover or recover from.

Use these dimensions to make project-specific choices. The alternatives are examples to combine or extend, not scores or required opposites. Choose from the brief and inspect the consequences; the user does not need to answer each dimension.

## interaction-01: Directness

**Alternatives:** Change a value / manipulate the object itself

**What changes:** Dragging, scrubbing or reshaping can make a relationship tangible.

**Apply to the brief:** Prefer manipulation of the represented relationship when it makes the idea clearer; retain accessible controls for values that require precision or non-pointer input.

## interaction-02: Resistance and momentum

**Alternatives:** Exact tracking / damped follow / inertia

**What changes:** A response can feel attached, weighted or loose.

**Apply to the brief:** Choose attached tracking, resistance and release momentum separately; softness should express material or intent while the user’s actions remain legible and responsive.

## interaction-03: Preview and commitment

**Alternatives:** Immediate action / peek / explicit commit

**What changes:** A temporary preview lets people explore without losing their place.

**Apply to the brief:** Offer reversible previews where exploration benefits from low commitment; make consequential commitment and cancellation clear.

## interaction-04: Affordance

**Alternatives:** Explicit control / suggestive object / hidden discovery

**What changes:** Novel interaction still needs a way to be found.

**Apply to the brief:** Match discovery to audience familiarity and task frequency; provide enough cues or an alternate route for essential actions to be found.

## interaction-05: Playfulness

**Alternatives:** Purely functional / fidgetable / game-like

**What changes:** A small response can reward curiosity without becoming a separate game.

**Apply to the brief:** Use play to reward curiosity or express character when the brief supports it; keep the primary job available and harmless experiments recoverable.

## interaction-06: Recovery

**Alternatives:** Undo / history / reversible state

**What changes:** Recoverability changes how freely someone can explore.

**Apply to the brief:** Choose undo, history or an explicit return path according to what can change; preserve enough state for exploration to feel safe and understandable.

## interaction-07: Feedback channel

**Alternatives:** Visual / haptic / optional sound

**What changes:** Different channels can reinforce a state without becoming mandatory.

**Apply to the brief:** Choose feedback channels for the state and platform, with user control and equivalent accessible information; sound or haptics should not be the sole essential cue.
