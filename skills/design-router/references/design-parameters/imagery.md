# Images & art direction

What is pictured, what is withheld, and how the frame changes the story.

Use these dimensions to make project-specific choices. The alternatives are examples to combine or extend, not scores or required opposites. Choose from the brief and inspect the consequences; the user does not need to answer each dimension.

## imagery-01: Subject distance

**Alternatives:** Intimate detail / human scale / distant world

**What changes:** Distance changes what the visitor is invited to notice.

**Apply to the brief:** Choose the distance that reveals what matters about the subject: a fragment can invite attention, while a wider view establishes situation or scale.

## imagery-02: Image treatment

**Alternatives:** Documentary / staged / abstract / synthetic

**What changes:** The image’s relationship to reality shapes the tone.

**Apply to the brief:** Select documentary, staged, abstract or synthetic treatment according to the project’s relationship to reality and the claims the imagery must support.

## imagery-03: Focal crop

**Alternatives:** Centred subject / displaced subject / empty field

**What changes:** Crop changes the relationship between the subject and surrounding copy.

**Apply to the brief:** Crop around the intended focal point and the space needed by surrounding content; check alternate aspect ratios rather than assuming the centre is always the subject.

## imagery-04: Sequence

**Alternatives:** Single hero / contact sheet / unfolding series

**What changes:** The same image collection can become a monument, archive or story.

**Apply to the brief:** Use a single image for concentration, a contact sheet for comparison or a sequence for development; let the audience’s job determine how the collection is encountered.

## imagery-05: Type-image relation

**Alternatives:** Caption / overprint / interlock

**What changes:** Type can explain an image, sit on it or become part of its shape.

**Apply to the brief:** Choose whether type explains, overlays or structurally joins the image; inspect contrast, reading order and the image’s important details in each state.

## imagery-06: Consistency and rupture

**Alternatives:** Uniform treatment / one deliberate exception

**What changes:** A controlled break can signal importance or a different register.

**Apply to the brief:** Create a recognisable treatment before using an exception to signal a meaningful change; preserve the identity of the collection through the interruption.
