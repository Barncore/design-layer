# Field guide sources

These 44 public references accompanied the original research dated 2026-09-20. They are routes to examples, mechanisms and evidence, not endorsements or a claim that every live experience was fully audited. Verify current implementation and platform facts at the primary source when using them.

The parameter catalogue is an original synthesis rather than a published taxonomy or a validated quality scale. Creator accounts describe their own work; practitioner advice carries judgment; studies answer their defined research questions. A source linked to a domain does not establish a causal claim for every parameter. Colour-emotion associations, for example, do not establish that a particular interface colour produces a particular feeling.
